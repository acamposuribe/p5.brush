Paint brushes & markers

Paint brushes (oil, gouache, ink, watercolour on a brush): bristles that spread with pressure, split into tufts and run out of paint as they go, skip over the paper when dry or barely touching, and pick up wet paint they cross and drag it along. Markers are the library's own, with two additions: a darker blob where the nib rests at the start and end, and flat (chisel) nibs. Both catch the paper's pits as small darker specks. Colours mix with the library's own spectral code. Paint brushes are prototype code; the markers are drawn by the library itself.
